MINARA SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION

System Overview

Minara is a state-of-the-art autonomous quantitative trading desk built specifically to execute and refine trading strategies without human intervention. Instead of running a sequence of disjointed scripts or relying on manual prompt-response cycles, Minara implements a complete closed-loop architecture where research, strategy development, and risk-checked execution continuously feed into one another.

System Goal: Move investing leverage away from speed or technical chart analysis and toward designing systematic feedback loops that run, backtest, scale, and protect themselves autonomously.

Loop Engineering Paradigm

Historically, software and AI interactions were designed as "open loops" (e.g. Chatbots). A human gives a prompt, the system responds, and the interaction ends. The human is the loop, manually verifying, copy-pasting, and making decisions.

Loop Engineering shifts the focus to "closed loops". The AI agent resides within an automated execution harness that continuously reads state, makes decisions, acts, and self-corrects without waiting for human prompts. By structuring these loops with strict verification gates, autonomous execution becomes secure and reliable.

Feature Prompt Engineering (Chat) Loop Engineering (Minara)
Execution One-off manual answers Recurring automated execution
Self-Correction None (relies on human feedback) Automatic testing & code verification
Risk Management Manual review of output Hard-coded, non-bypassable limits

The 5-Beat Anatomy of a Loop

Every autonomous loop in Minara follows a deterministic five-beat lifecycle. If any beat fails or fails to verify, the loop halts and alerts the risk layer:

  1. Trigger: The scheduling or event hook that starts the loop (e.g., U.S. market pre-open, price crossing a threshold, or a cron timer).
  2. Observe: The ingestion stage. The agent pulls multi-domain market data (on-chain flows, order books, funding rate bias, sentiment charts) to establish current state.
  3. Decide: The reasoning stage. The agent evaluates the observed facts against the active thesis parameters, calculating size, direction, and entry limit.
  4. Act: The execution stage. The order is securely routed to the API interface (e.g. Hyperliquid perpetuals margin pools).
  5. Verify: The safety gate. The agent verifies order fill status, checks that Stop-Loss (SL) and Take-Profit (TP) conditions are correctly attached, and audits slippage limits.

Loop 1: Research Loop (Steps 01-04)

The Research Loop acts as the information ingestion engine. It translates macro hunches and raw data feeds into structured investment theses.

Steps Breakdown:

  • 01. Ask the Market: Enter targeted questions to extract macro biases. The output is structured JSON-like key/value parameters (e.g., correlation indexes or regime states).
  • 02. Deep-dive Assets: Inspect target tokens across six data domains (Markets, On-chain, Signals, Derivatives, Predictions, and DeFi).
  • 03. Audit Trade History: Evaluate sizing and timing leaks. The AI scans past fills to identify psychological leaks (e.g., buying too large, trading during low-liquidity periods).
  • 04. Thesis Compilation: Consolidate the findings into a formalized, machine-readable strategy specification.

Loop 2: Strategy Studio (Steps 05-08)

The Strategy Studio takes the compiled research thesis and translates it into formal risk-model rules that can be backtested and paper traded.

Steps Breakdown:

  • 05. Rule Definition: Convert plain-text rules or templates into structured entry and exit scripts.
  • 06. Template Selection: Start from proven templates including Momentum, Mean-reversion, Arbitrage, or Cointegrated Pairs.
  • 07. Premium Backtesting: Replay the strategy against years of tick-level data, incorporating execution slippage, exchange fees, and funding rate borrow skews.
  • 08. Paper Trading Verification: Run the strategy against live market feeds using simulated balance sheets before deploying real capital.
Leakage Test Gate: Backtests must clear out-of-sample data leakage checks before the system enables the live promotion path.

Loop 3: Execution Loop (Steps 09-12)

The Execution Loop acts as the hands-off engine that manages live orders, monitors active risk limits, and reports back P&L outcomes.

Steps Breakdown:

  • 09. Workflow Scheduling: Create triggers to automate recurring tasks (e.g., run pre-open reports daily, check volume anomalies hourly).
  • 10. Alert-Only Dry Runs: Let the loop watch and alert without execution permission to verify edge cases.
  • 11. Autopilot Engagement: Empower the engine to route trades directly to decentralized or centralized pools using strict API hooks.
  • 12. Compounding & Review: Close the loop by feeding execution metrics back into the Research Loop to refine strategy parameters.

Durable Risk Safeguards

Autonomous trading is only safe if risk boundaries cannot be bypassed by the AI itself. Minara implements four non-negotiable hard-coded rules at the contract/API connection level:

  • Leverage Limits: Globally enforced margin multipliers (e.g., 5.0x max) that scale down orders if leverage exceeds boundaries.
  • Equity Drawdown Kill-switch: If total account equity drops by the defined threshold (e.g. 5% floor) from high-water mark, all open positions are flattened immediately and Autopilot disables itself.
  • Asset Scope Restriction: The engine is whitelisted to trade only specific tokens ($SOL, $HYPE) and will ignore all other markets.
  • Mandatory Stop-Loss & Take-Profit: Orders cannot exist on-exchange without attached exit limits.
